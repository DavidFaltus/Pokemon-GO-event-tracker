## Executive Summary

As of **2026**, the Pokémon GO PvE (Raid & Gym) attacker meta is characterized by:
1. **Fusion & Signature Form Supremacy**: **Dusk Mane Necrozma** (*Sunsteel Strike*) and **Dawn Wings Necrozma** (*Moongeist Beam*) reign as the pinnacle non-Mega attackers in the entire game, matching or exceeding top Mega/Primal forms in neutral and super-effective DPS.
2. **Mega & Primal Apex Tier**: **Mega Rayquaza** (*Dragon Ascent*), **Primal Groudon** (*Precipice Blades*), **Primal Kyogre** (*Origin Pulse*), and **Mega Lucario** (*Force Palm + Aura Sphere*) remain the absolute best in their respective types.
3. **Adventure Effects & Special Mechanics**: Signature moves like *Roar of Time* (Dialga Origin - pause timers), *Spacial Rend* (Palkia Origin - expand map radius), *Sunsteel Strike* (attract daytime spawns/evolutions), and *Moongeist Beam* (attract nighttime spawns/evolutions) provide utility beyond combat.
4. **Paldea & Galar Integration**: Major Paldea staples (**Baxcalibur**, **Annihilape**, **Gholdengo**, **Ceruledge**, **Armarouge**, Paldean Starters) and Galar heavyweights (**Urshifu Single/Rapid Strike**, **Kingambit**, Galar Starters, **Dreepy/Dragapult**) are fully released and integrated into the meta, while Paradox Pokémon (e.g. *Great Tusk, Flutter Mane, Roaring Moon*) and Ruinous Legendaries (*Chien-Pao, Chi-Yu, Ting-Lu, Wo-Chien, Koraidon, Miraidon*) remain unreleased.
5. **Standardized Evaluation Metric**: The **DialgaDex Equivalent Rating (ER = $\sqrt[4]{\text{DPS}^3 \times \text{TDO}}$)** is the definitive gold standard for ranking PvE attackers, perfectly balancing high burst damage against survivability and re-lobbying penalties.
